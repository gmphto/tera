# Local loudness measurements

`backend.analysis.loudness.measure_loudness(LoadedAudio)` returns an immutable
`LoudnessMeasurements` record containing `analysis_version` and four existing
contract `Measurement` objects. No schema or dependency change is needed.

```python
from backend.audio import load_wav
from backend.analysis.loudness import measure_loudness

audio = load_wav("samples/kick.wav")
result = measure_loudness(audio)
for item in result.measurements:
    print(item.name, item.value, item.unit, item.unavailable_reason)
```

## Units and channel aggregation

RMS is the square root of the mean square across all frames and channels;
sample peak is the greatest absolute sample across either channel. Both use
linear full-scale amplitude. Crest factor is peak/RMS, a dimensionless ratio.
This is sample peak, not true peak. Finite overrange floating-point input is
measured without clipping. Opposite-polarity channels do not cancel: waveforms
are never downmixed. Samples, reader metadata and source files remain unchanged.

LUFS uses separately K-weighted channels. Mono, left and right each have unit
energy weight. The implementation follows the programme-loudness path of
[ITU-R BS.1770-5, Annex 1](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/bs/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I!!PDF-E.pdf):
400 ms blocks, 100 ms hop, reference offset -0.691, an absolute threshold of
-70 LUFS, then a threshold 10 LU below the absolute-gated energy mean. Both
comparisons are strict `>`; the final selection must clear both thresholds.
Incomplete tail blocks are discarded. There is no padding or clip repetition.

The two 48 kHz filter sections use the published Tables 1/2 coefficients
verbatim in `loudness.py`. For 44.1 and 96 kHz, invert the reference bilinear
substitution `z=(96000+s)/(96000-s)` and discretize the resulting analogue
transfer using `scipy.signal.bilinear` at the target rate. Cascaded filtering
uses zero initial state, with no warmup exclusion and no state carried between
clips. Independent response checks cover 40, 100, 1000 and 10000 Hz at all
three rates. This does not claim a live meter or full EBU Mode compliance.

## Availability and numerical limits

Input must be a nonempty floating-point array shaped `(frames, 1 or 2)` in a
`LoadedAudio` whose channel/frame metadata and positive integer sample rate
agree. Nonfinite samples, invalid shapes/rates/duration or empty arrays raise
`ValueError`; they do not produce contract measurements.

Availability precedence:

1. Exact digital silence: RMS/peak zero; crest and loudness unavailable with
   `silent_audio`, even when short or at an unsupported loudness rate.
2. Other clips below 400 ms: loudness unavailable with
   `too_short_for_integrated_loudness`.
3. Other rates than 44.1/48/96 kHz: loudness unavailable with
   `unsupported_loudness_sample_rate`; linear measurements remain available.
4. No blocks above the absolute gate: `below_loudness_gate`.

All unknown measurements have `value=None`, a reason, and no confidence.
No NaN/infinity is returned. Known DSP measurements also omit confidence.

To avoid overflow from squaring a large finite float64 sample, arithmetic uses
samples divided by their absolute peak. RMS restores that scale; LUFS adds
`20*log10(peak)` to the filtered energy level. This is internal numerical
conditioning, not a modification or output normalization of audio. Crest is
computed from the scale-free RMS. The tested range includes the maximum finite
float64 and its smallest positive subnormal. If a nonzero mathematical RMS
underflows float64, only RMS becomes unknown with `numerical_range`; peak and
scale-free crest remain valid. Tiny gated signals have unknown loudness rather
than negative infinity. Contributions beyond float64 precision may round away;
this is not arbitrary-precision metering.

## Independent calibration evidence

`tests/loudness_reference_signals.py` constructs original in-phase stereo
1 kHz sines using per-channel peak amplitude `10**(dBFS/20)` and exact rounded
frame counts. Segment specifications and expected integrated readings come
from [EBU Tech 3341 (2023), Table 1, cases 1–5](https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf).
They are not generated by the extractor. No audio download or network access
is needed for tests. Each segment starts at zero phase; lengths are integer
numbers of 1 kHz cycles. No fades, normalization or calibration offsets are
applied.

| Case | Sequential seconds at dBFS | Published LUFS | Observed at 48 kHz |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 20 at -23 | -23 | -22.993297 |
| 2 | 20 at -33 | -33 | -32.993297 |
| 3 | 10 at -36; 60 at -23; 10 at -36 | -23 | -23.013869 |
| 4 | 10 at -72; case 3; 10 at -72 | -23 | -23.013869 |
| 5 | 20 at -26; 20.1 at -20; 20 at -26 | -23 | -22.978657 |

All are within the published ±0.1 LU tolerance. Rate-equivalent cases 1/4
measure -22.992708/-23.013279 at 44.1 kHz and -22.995684/-23.016256 at 96 kHz,
also within ±0.1 LU of -23. These are rate-equivalent local experiments, not
additional published EBU files. Independent frequency-response expectations
evaluate the published 48 kHz rational transfer directly with complex numbers;
no production filter builder is called to calculate those expectations.

Analytic tests additionally verify constant, sampled-sine, impulse and scaling
identities; mono/stereo/unequal/opposite-polarity channels; silence and gates;
one sample below/at/above 400 ms; finite extremes; source preservation; and
contract serialization. Linear analytic tolerance is relative 1e-13 or tighter;
frequency-response LUFS tolerance is 0.1 LU. Stereo duplication adds
3.0102999566 LU when gates match.

## Versioned Sample integration

The extractor's version is `loudness-bs1770-5-v1`. Change it when coefficients,
supported rate policy, gates, block rules, aggregation or numerical availability
semantics change. A version bump invalidates previously cached measurements;
it does not change the shared schema version. Other extractors remain unknown
until implemented, as this example and its executable contract test show:

```python
from pathlib import Path
from backend.contracts import (
    Sample, AudioMetadata, AudioFeatures, Measurement, MusicalKey, MEASURES,
)

path = Path("samples/kick.wav").resolve()
audio = load_wav(path)
result = measure_loudness(audio)
known_names = {m.name for m in result.measurements}
unknowns = tuple(
    Measurement(name=name, value=None, unit=spec[0],
                unavailable_reason="not_implemented")
    for name, spec in MEASURES.items() if name not in known_names
)
sample = Sample(
    sample_id="kick-example", role="kick", analysis_version=result.analysis_version,
    audio=AudioMetadata(local_path=str(path), sample_rate_hz=audio.sample_rate_hz,
                        channels=audio.channels, frame_count=audio.frame_count,
                        duration_ms=audio.duration_seconds * 1000),
    features=AudioFeatures(
        measurements=result.measurements + unknowns,
        key=MusicalKey(tonic=None, mode=None, confidence=None,
                       unavailable_reason="not_implemented")),
)
```

The path and measurements stay local. Ranking and compatibility judgments are
outside this callable.
